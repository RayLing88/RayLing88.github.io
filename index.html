Lei Ling

PhD Candidate, College of Biology, Hunan University

Lei Ling is a PhD candidate in the College of Biology at Hunan University. His research interests include AI for science, genome language models, statistical genetics, machine learning, deep learning, and bioinformatics.

Recent work spans explicit cross-strand interactions for DNA sequence language modelling, plant microRNA annotation, and species distribution modelling under climate change.
